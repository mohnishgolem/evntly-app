# SYNTHETIC legal research question

Evntly (a Sydney marketplace connecting customers with event vendors) wants to:

1. Email Sydney photographers whose business email is published on their own
   website, inviting them to list on Evntly.
2. Show "Founding Vendor" badges and say "Sydney's fastest-growing event
   marketplace" on the homepage.
3. Take a 10% booking commission and hold the customer's deposit until the
   event date.

Question: what Australian (Commonwealth and NSW) laws apply to each, what are
the key obligations, and where are the primary sources?
