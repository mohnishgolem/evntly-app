# Customer acquisition: the first 10 genuine enquiries

**24 Nov 2026 · draft for Sai's approval. Nothing has been posted, sent or spent.**

## The problem in one line
8 complete vendor listings, **0 genuine enquiries ever** (all 6 quote requests went to demo listings, 5
from an admin). 14 customer accounts and 10 event plans exist, but none reached a real vendor. A
marketplace only exists when a customer and a vendor transact; until then every vendor we add is a
promise we haven't kept.

## Principle: do the matching by hand
Airbnb's founders photographed apartments themselves. Evntly's version is **the founder as
matchmaker**: for every real person planning an event in Sydney, Mohnish personally helps them find
2–3 suitable Evntly vendors and send a quote request *through Evntly*. It doesn't scale, and it
doesn't need to yet: 10 enquiries is 10 conversations.

Hard rules: no fake enquiries, bookings, reviews, testimonials, likes or comments; disclose that you
run Evntly every time you recommend it; follow every group's rules; incentives never tied to reviews
(ACCC: incentivised reviews must be disclosed, and incentives for positive-only reviews risk misleading
consumers).

## Where Phase 1 demand comes from (ranked: free and fast first)

| # | Channel | What exactly | Cost | Why it should work |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **People already in Evntly** | The 14 customer accounts and 10 event plans: a personal note offering free help finding vendors for *their* event (date, suburb already known). Customers who started a plan asked for help. | $0 | Warmest possible audience. Needs your OK per message (customer contact). |
| 2 | **Vendor "date-taken" referrals** | Each complete vendor turns down enquiries (date clash, outside their style). Ask them to send those people to their Evntly peers: a short link `evntly…/explore?src=vendor_referral` or the peer's profile. They lose nothing and help a friend. | $0 | Vendors already have demand they can't serve; it's the most trusted recommendation a couple gets. |
| 3 | **Founder's personal network** | Friends, family, colleagues, cultural and community networks: "Who do you know planning a wedding, 30th, engagement or work event this summer?" One personal message each. | $0 | Fast, honest, and tests whether the product helps real planners. |
| 4 | **Event-feed posts** | Help each real planner post their event on Evntly's event feed so *all* matching vendors can apply. One post can produce several genuine enquiries from vendors' side. | $0 | Uses an existing feature; vendors see a real lead the same day. |
| 5 | **Local Facebook community & wedding groups** | Answer "recommendations for a DJ / cake / photographer?" posts with 2–3 genuine vendor suggestions *including non-Evntly ones where they fit*, plus "I run Evntly, a new Sydney marketplace" disclosure. Never cold-post promotions; read each group's rules first (many ban promotion or restrict vendors to set threads). | $0, ~30 min/day | Easy Weddings' own guidance: helpers are received better than pitchers; admins remove rule-breakers. |
| 6 | **BYO-vendor venues** | Community halls, function rooms, restaurants with private rooms and venues that *allow outside suppliers*. Offer a free, genuine "vendors who've worked here / serve this area" page and a QR card for their enquiries. Start with venues near the 8 vendors. | $0–$50 printing | Venues are asked "who do you recommend?" every week; many publish preferred-supplier lists. |
| 7 | **Instagram organic** | (a) Founding Vendor features already promised — make each a **collab post** with the vendor so it reaches their followers. (b) Planning Reels: "what a 30th in Parramatta costs", "5 questions to ask a wedding DJ". Every post points to the free planner. | $0 | Collab posts borrow real audiences; the planner turns a viewer into a plan. |
| 8 | **Google / local SEO** | `/sydney/[category]` pages exist. Add genuinely useful planning pages that link to the planner and those pages: e.g. "21st birthday checklist Sydney", "engagement party ideas Sydney", "how much does a wedding DJ cost in Sydney". Ask vendors to link their Evntly profile from their own website and Instagram bio (`/vendor-dashboard/share`). Note: Google Business Profile is meant for businesses serving customers in person; an online-only marketplace is likely ineligible, so don't create one for Evntly. | $0 | Compounds; months, not weeks. Start now. |
| 9 | **Customer referral incentive** | *Phase 2, after the first bookings:* a small "give & get" thank-you for referring a friend who **sends a genuine enquiry**, disclosed, never conditional on a review or rating. Get a short legal check first. | Small | Only worth it once there's a product people recommend. |
| 10 | **Paid search** | *Phase 2, only once channels 1–6 show enquiry → booking works:* a capped Google Search test on high-intent long-tail ("DJ for 21st Sydney"), measured by genuine enquiries per dollar. | Capped, needs approval | Highest intent of any paid channel; still pointless without proof of conversion. |

Seasonal: wedding expos (e.g. a Rosehill expo listed for 7 Mar 2027 and a Randwick one on 7 Feb 2027 — confirm with organisers) are a Phase 2 option to *walk* (talk to couples and vendors), not to buy a stand.

## Phase 1 operating rhythm (founder, ~2 hours a day)

| Daily | Weekly |
|---|---|
| 30 min: answer 3–5 genuine recommendation requests in local groups (disclosed) | Personal note to every new customer account and event plan (with approval) |
| 30 min: matchmaking — help each active planner shortlist vendors and send requests | One venue visit or call |
| 30 min: vendor side — tell vendors about real leads, ask for date-taken referrals | Two Instagram collab posts with vendors |
| 30 min: Growth dashboard + reply speed check (every enquiry answered within 24 h) | Review dashboard: which channel produced enquiries? |

## Milestones and measures (Admin → Growth)

| Phase 1 goal | Measure |
|---|---|
| All 12 approved vendors complete | Completed listings (12) |
| 20 completed listings | Completed listings |
| First 10 genuine enquiries | Genuine enquiries (real customer → real, non-demo vendor) |
| First confirmed booking | Confirmed bookings |
| Each complete vendor gets ≥ 1 enquiry | "Approved vendors with an enquiry" |

Phase 2 (50 completed, repeatable channels, CAC): log every dollar in the spend log; judge each channel
on **cost per genuine enquiry and per confirmed booking**, not clicks or sign-ups.

## Gaps to close (small, recommended next)
1. **Customer-side source tracking.** Today only founding pages and vendor share links record where a
   visitor came from. Tag customer links (`?src=fb_group`, `venue_qr`, `vendor_referral`, `ig_collab`) and
   record them on page views and quote requests, so the dashboard can say which channel produced each
   enquiry. Needs a small migration.
2. **Enquiry response time** on the dashboard (first vendor reply to a genuine enquiry), because the first
   enquiry that goes unanswered loses the customer and the vendor.
